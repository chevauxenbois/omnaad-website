Questions

What founders usually ask.

How early should I book?

The earlier the better. Diwali, Christmas and New Year's Eve are busy for everyone, so talk to us a few weeks before the season starts.

Will it damage my walls or finishes?

No. Every setup is designed to install and come down without damaging your space.

Can we use the decor again next year?

Yes. We build setups to be stored and reused, so each year costs less.

Can you do a one-off private event?

Yes. Launches, anniversaries, private parties and team evenings are all part of what we set up.

Is this a good way to try working with you?

Yes. A festive setup is the easiest way to see how we work before a bigger project.
