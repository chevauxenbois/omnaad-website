What we design

Ambience lives two lives.

Some of it is built to last for years. Some of it lasts a night or a season, and keeps the place feeling new.

Permanent

The space itself

  • New design. You've signed a lease or bought a place, and need it planned from the first idea to opening night.
  • Renovation. The interior has aged, the theme feels flat, or the crowd you want isn't coming in.
Ephemeral

The moments in it

  • Festive and themed. Ganeshotsav, Navratri, Diwali, Christmas, New Year's Eve, monsoon, IPL nights.
  • Events. Live music, stand-up, brunches, launches and private parties, set up and taken down cleanly.
Before you build anything

Four questions every founder should answer.

01
Who exactly is this place for?
"Everyone" is not a crowd. A clear guest shapes every choice that follows.
02
Will people want to photograph it?
In Pune, discovery happens on Instagram. A space that gets shared markets itself.
03
Will you host events and shows?
If yes, the layout, sound and lighting need to plan for it from day one.
04
Is it designed with intent, or just "pretty"?
Pretty is forgettable. Intent is what makes a place feel like yours.

01
Discovery

We start by listening.

A focused session, on site wherever possible, to understand what you're building and for whom.

You getA one-page ambience scorecard and a clear recommendation for where to begin.
Your vision
The story behind the brand, and the feeling you want guests to leave with.
Your guest
Age, occasion, group size and spend. Weekday crowds and weekend crowds are often different people.
Your numbers
Budget band, timeline, expected spend per guest and how long they should stay.
Your site
Lease terms, landlord and society limits, terrace use, noise and signage rules.

What people love

What gets shared

What's worth it

03
Design brief

Six decisions that shape every visit.

You getA signed-off brief, moodboards for each zone, a guest journey map and a budget split by zone.
Kinds of spaces
Entry, bar, communal tables, booths, terrace, private dining, a stage.
Customer journey
Arrive, wait, sit, order, eat, pay, leave, and the photo on the way out.
Highlight points
The entry moment, a hero wall, a signature photo spot, even the washroom.
Materials & textures
Local stone, terracotta, cane and wood, chosen to survive Pune's monsoon.
Lighting & mood
Separate day and night scenes. Warm, dimmable, never flat.
Architecture
Layout, movement, acoustics, and how kitchen and bar meet the floor.

Our approach
Every great restaurant tells a story. We imagine your space as a character, and then we build its world.

Before we draw a single line, we ask what the space is trying to say. What world does this character live in? What does it love to eat and drink? What does it like to be around, and who is it becoming?

Slowly the character comes alive. It becomes a moodboard of how the space should feel, a palette of the woods, stones, fabrics and finishes it would choose, and a set of colours that carries the mood.

When guests walk through your door, they step into a different world for an evening. That is what makes people stay longer, share it with friends, and come back.
