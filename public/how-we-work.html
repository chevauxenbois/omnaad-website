01
Discovery

We start by listening.

A focused session, on site wherever possible, to understand what you're building and for whom.

You getA one-page ambience scorecard and a clear recommendation for where to begin.
Your vision
The story behind the brand, and the feeling you want guests to leave with.
Your guest
Age, occasion, group size and spend. Weekday crowds and weekend crowds are often different people.
Your numbers
Budget band, timeline, expected spend per guest and how long they should stay.
Your site
Lease terms, landlord and society limits, terrace use, noise and signage rules.
02
Market study

Know your crowd before you draw a line.

At a bar, the vibe matters most. At a restaurant, food and ambience matter together.

You getA crowd profile, a look at nearby competitors, and two or three theme directions to choose from.
Global trends
Formats, materials and lighting ideas working in cities around the world.
What people love
The music, aesthetics and activities your guests are into right now.
What gets shared
The corners, plates and moments that show up in posts and Reels.
What's worth it
Why guests return, and why they recommend a place to friends.
Your neighbourhood
The venues around you, and the gap your place can fill.
Market study · Pune

Every part of Pune has its own crowd.

Koregaon Park · Kalyani Nagar

Nightlife, premium dining, young professionals and expats.
Mood, music, late-night energy

Baner · Balewadi · Aundh

IT professionals and young families, high-street and rooftop dining.
Comfort, terraces, weekend brunch

Viman Nagar · Kharadi

Students and IT teams mixing on the same street.
Value and vibe in equal measure

FC Road · JM Road

College crowd, café hopping, budget-conscious.
Highly shareable, quick turnover

Camp · Old Pune

Heritage, nostalgia and long-loved food culture.
Character over trend

Hinjewadi · Wakad

Weekday lunches, team outings, Friday after-work.
Groups, speed, celebrations
03
Design brief

Six decisions that shape every visit.

You getA signed-off brief, moodboards for each zone, a guest journey map and a budget split by zone.
Kinds of spaces
Entry, bar, communal tables, booths, terrace, private dining, a stage.
Customer journey
Arrive, wait, sit, order, eat, pay, leave, and the photo on the way out.
Highlight points
The entry moment, a hero wall, a signature photo spot, even the washroom.
Materials & textures
Local stone, terracotta, cane and wood, chosen to survive Pune's monsoon.
Lighting & mood
Separate day and night scenes. Warm, dimmable, never flat.
Architecture
Layout, movement, acoustics, and how kitchen and bar meet the floor.
04
Placemaking

From drawings to a finished space.

One team, accountable from the first sketch to the final styled corner.

You getA complete drawing set, 3D views, an approved budget and schedule, and a finished, styled space.
Layout & zoning
Where everything goes, and why.
Drawings & 3D views
See the space before a single wall is touched.
Budget & vendors
Itemised quantities, quotes and a trusted local vendor bench.
Site execution
Weekly site visits and updates, with wet work planned around the monsoon.
Styling & handover
Art, plants, props and signage placed, then a final walk-through.
05 Collaborations · 06 Launch

Give people a reason to talk, from the first night.

Collaborations

Things no competitor can copy

  • Artists and designers: murals, installations, tableware, lettering
  • Partner brands: co-hosted nights with local breweries, roasters and labels
  • Performers: musicians, DJs, stand-up and open-mic collectives
  • Creators: Pune's food and lifestyle voices, invited early
Launch or relaunch

An opening that gets shared

  • Soft open for friends, family and regulars to test the flow
  • A creator preview evening with every photo spot lit and styled
  • A public launch built around a collaborator
  • A professional photo and video kit you can use for months
07
Operations

Ambience your team can run every day.

A beautiful space fades if no one knows how to run it. We hand over the playbook and track what's working.

Lighting by the hour
Lunch, sunset and late-night scenes, set and labelled.
Music & volume
The right sound for each part of the day.
Upkeep
Plants, props, art and photo spots kept looking new.
Event changeover
A simple checklist to turn the room for a show night.
What we measure
Tagged posts, ambience mentions in reviews, quiet-day footfall, spend per guest and event bookings, checked at 30, 60 and 90 days.
