What we look at

An honest look, in person and online.

On site

Peak hour and quiet hour.

We walk the full guest journey twice: on a busy weekend evening and a slow weekday afternoon.

  • Arrival, waiting, seating, ordering, leaving
  • Lighting and mood, day and night
  • Flow, noise and comfort

  • Google and Zomato reviews that mention ambience
  • Photo spots that exist, and ones that are missing

What's possible

Small changes that can change a room.

Lighting

From flat to warm

Swapping bright overhead light for warm, dimmable scenes can turn a café that empties at 7 pm into an evening place.

A photo moment

One wall worth sharing

A single well-lit feature near the entrance can give guests a reason to post, and their friends a reason to visit.

Layout

A room for the crowd you want

Reworking one zone for groups, a bar counter or a small stage can open the door to a new crowd.

These are ideas to show what's possible, not past projects.

Questions

What founders usually ask.

Do I have to close while you audit?

No. We visit during normal service, once at a busy hour and once at a quiet one, so we see the place as guests do.

Will you tell me to renovate everything?

No. The report is split by effort: quick wins first, then bigger fixes, and a renovation only if the space really needs it.

What do you need from me?

Access at two times of day, a short conversation with you or your manager, and links to your Instagram and review pages.

Can you also carry out the fixes?

Yes. We can deliver the quick wins and bigger fixes ourselves, or hand the report to your own team.

What does an audit cost?

It depends on the size of the venue. We share the fee after a short call about your place.
