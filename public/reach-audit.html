Signs you need one

Good food, but something's missing.

01
Nothing about the place stands out.
Guests like it, but can't say what makes it different from the café next door.
02
The vibe feels flat, or worse, gloomy.
Lighting, music and layout are working against the mood you want.
03
The crowd you want isn't coming in.
A Gen Z crowd looking for energy and photo moments walks into what feels like a family diner.
04
The interior has simply aged.
Five to ten years of wear, and a look that has stopped working.
What we look at

An honest look, in person and online.

On site

Peak hour and quiet hour.

We walk the full guest journey twice: on a busy weekend evening and a slow weekday afternoon.

  • Arrival, waiting, seating, ordering, leaving
  • Lighting and mood, day and night
  • Flow, noise and comfort
Online

What guests say and share.

Your tagged posts and reviews show what guests notice, and what they don't.

  • Instagram tags and location posts
  • Google and Zomato reviews that mention ambience
  • Photo spots that exist, and ones that are missing
