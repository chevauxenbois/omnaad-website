Key highlights

Four things every new space gets.

Theme setting

One clear vibe.

We turn your brand story and your guest into a single theme, so every choice that follows pulls in the same direction.

  • A crowd profile and a look at nearby competitors
  • Two or three theme directions to choose from
  • Moodboards for each zone
Space detailing for socials

Moments worth sharing.

In Pune, discovery happens on Instagram. We plan the photo moments on purpose instead of hoping they appear.

  • An entry moment and a hero wall
  • A signature photo spot, lit for night as well as day
  • Even the washroom, which gets photographed more than you'd think
Design detailing

Built for event nights and everyday guests.

The same room has to work for a quiet Tuesday lunch and a packed Saturday show.

  • Flexible seating, stage and sound planned from day one
  • Lighting scenes for lunch, sunset and late night
  • Materials chosen to survive Pune's monsoon
