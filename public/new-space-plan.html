Key highlights

Four things every new space gets.

Theme setting

One clear vibe.

We turn your brand story and your guest into a single theme, so every choice that follows pulls in the same direction.

  • A crowd profile and a look at nearby competitors
  • Two or three theme directions to choose from
  • Moodboards for each zone
Space detailing for socials

Moments worth sharing.

In Pune, discovery happens on Instagram. We plan the photo moments on purpose instead of hoping they appear.

  • An entry moment and a hero wall
  • A signature photo spot, lit for night as well as day
  • Even the washroom, which gets photographed more than you'd think
Design detailing

Built for event nights and everyday guests.

The same room has to work for a quiet Tuesday lunch and a packed Saturday show.

  • Flexible seating, stage and sound planned from day one
  • Lighting scenes for lunch, sunset and late night
  • Materials chosen to survive Pune's monsoon

Questions

What founders usually ask.

When should I bring you in?

As early as you can, ideally before you sign the lease or start drawings. Site limits such as terrace use, noise rules and structure shape the design, and changes cost less on paper than on site.

Do you get involved in the menu or kitchen?

No. You own the food and drinks. We design around your menu and work alongside your chef or kitchen consultant so the space and the food tell the same story.

Do you only design, or do you also build?

Both. We take the project from drawings and 3D views to vendor selection, weekly site visits and final styling, so one team stays accountable.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the size of the space and how much we take on. After the private consultation we give you a budget band, split by zone, before you commit to anything.

What happens after we open?

You get an ambience playbook for your team, reviews at 30, 60 and 90 days, and the option of a refresh every quarter.
